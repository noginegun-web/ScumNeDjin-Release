# Governance

ScumNeDjin is maintainer-led. `noginegun-web` is the project steward and makes
the final decision on roadmap, accepted pull requests, release tags, supported
configurations and additional maintainers.

Contributions are welcome through pull requests. Acceptance depends on scope,
tests, security, documentation, license compatibility and long-term
maintainability. Forks remain free to develop under the license, while official
releases are issued only by the project steward.

Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Community conduct follows
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
