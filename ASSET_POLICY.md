# Asset and data policy

Only material that the contributor created or is expressly licensed to
redistribute may be committed to this repository.

Do not add:

- game files, packages, extracts, screenshots, maps, icons or dumps;
- databases, player records, logs, crash reports or backups;
- content copied from another project without its license and attribution;
- credentials, access keys, private URLs or personal data.

Use the empty catalog templates and local ignored directories for installation
specific material. A pull request that adds data or media must identify its
origin, license and redistribution permission.
