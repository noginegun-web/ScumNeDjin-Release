# Project name and logo policy

The names **ScumNeDjin**, associated logo and the designation **Official** are
project identifiers stewarded by `noginegun-web`. The source-code license does
not grant permission to imply endorsement, affiliation or official status.

You may accurately state that a fork is "based on ScumNeDjin" and link to the
canonical project at https://github.com/noginegun-web/ScumNeDjin-Release.

For a modified public build, use a distinct primary name and clearly identify
it as an unofficial fork unless `noginegun-web` grants written permission. Do
not use the project logo or the word **Official** in a way that suggests the
original author tested, supports or released your modified build.

This policy is not a trademark-registration claim and does not limit truthful
references to the project.
